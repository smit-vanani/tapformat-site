Tapformat 1.1LATEST

October 27, 2026

A redesign of nearly everything you see.

  • A cinematic welcome. It teaches you the shortcut by having you press it, and sets up everything in one pass.
  • Your own shortcut. Keep ⇧⌘ or record anything: a tap like ⌃⇧, or keys like ⌥Space.
  • Drag & drop. Drop files on the Tapformat window and the ring opens.
  • Finds your file. The converted file is selected in Finder as soon as it's ready.
  • Fewer folder prompts. The welcome asks for Desktop, Documents and Downloads in one go, and Settings links to Full Disk Access to stop prompts entirely.
  • New Settings and menu bar. Everything in one place, with a count of how many files you've converted.

Tapformat 1.0.2

October 22, 2026
  • Smaller updates. Updates now download only what changed, a few hundred KB instead of the whole app.
  • Anonymous usage count. Just the number of files converted, never the files or their names. You can turn it off in Settings.
